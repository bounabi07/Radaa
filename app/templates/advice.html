Apps & Downloads

Stay safe when downloading and using applications

Download only from official stores

Always use Google Play or App Store. Avoid third-party APK websites — they often contain malware hidden inside apps.

Check app permissions

A flashlight app doesn't need access to your contacts or camera. Review permissions before installing any app.

Check reviews and ratings

Low rating or few reviews on a new app can be a red flag. Read recent reviews carefully before downloading.

Keep apps updated

Updates patch security vulnerabilities. Enable automatic updates to stay protected against known exploits.

Avoid cracked or modded apps

Modified apps (cracked versions) bypass security checks and may steal your data, passwords, or banking information.

Password Security

Protect your accounts with strong, unique passwords

Use a strong password

Use at least 12 characters mixing uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Avoid names, birthdays, or common words.

Never reuse passwords

If one account is breached, attackers try the same password on all your accounts. Use a unique password for each site.

Use a password manager

Tools like Bitwarden or 1Password generate and store complex passwords securely so you only need to remember one.

Enable two-factor authentication (2FA)

Even if your password is stolen, 2FA blocks attackers. Enable it on email, banking, and social media accounts.

Change passwords after a breach

Use Radaa's email scanner to check if your email appeared in a data breach, then immediately change those passwords.

Phishing & Scams

Recognize and avoid online fraud attempts

Don't click suspicious links

Hover over links before clicking to see the real URL. If it looks strange or uses URL shorteners, use Radaa's link scanner first.

Beware of urgency tricks

"Your account will be suspended in 24 hours!" is a classic pressure tactic. Legitimate companies never rush you like this.

If it sounds too good to be true...

"You won $5000!" or "Free iPhone!" are scam traps. Never enter personal info on prize claim pages.

Verify sender identity

Scammers impersonate Netflix, PayPal, or your bank. Always check the sender's email domain carefully for slight misspellings.

Use Radaa to analyze suspicious messages

Copy any suspicious SMS or email and paste it in Radaa's message detector for instant AI-powered analysis.

Social Media Safety

Protect your privacy on social platforms

Limit personal information

Don't share your phone number, address, or ID publicly. Scammers use this info to target and manipulate you.

Be cautious with friend requests

Fake accounts are used to gather information or spread scams. Verify identity before accepting requests from strangers.

Review privacy settings

Set your profile to private. Regularly check who can see your posts, location, and personal information.

Beware of money transfer requests

If a "friend" suddenly asks for money via DM, verify by calling them directly. Account hacking for money fraud is very common.

Enable login alerts

Turn on notifications for new logins on all your accounts. You'll be instantly alerted if someone else accesses your profile.

WiFi & Network Security

Stay safe on public and private networks

Avoid public WiFi for sensitive tasks

Never do online banking or enter passwords on café or airport WiFi. Attackers can intercept unencrypted traffic easily.

Use a VPN on public networks

A VPN encrypts your connection and hides your activity from attackers on the same network. Use Proton VPN (free tier available).

Verify the network name

Attackers create fake hotspots named "Free_Airport_WiFi". Always confirm the exact network name with staff before connecting.

Secure your home router

Change the default router password. Use WPA3 or WPA2 encryption. Disable WPS which is vulnerable to brute force attacks.

Email Security

Protect your inbox from threats and spam

Never open unexpected attachments

Malware is often distributed via email attachments (.exe, .zip, .docx with macros). Use Radaa's file scanner before opening.

Check the sender's email address

Scammers use addresses like "support@paypa1.com" (with a 1 instead of l). Always check carefully for subtle misspellings.

Don't unsubscribe from spam blindly

Clicking "unsubscribe" on a spam email confirms your address is active. Just mark it as spam and delete it instead.

Check if your email was breached

Use Radaa's email scanner to see if your address appeared in known data breaches and take action immediately if it did.

Use email filters and spam settings

Enable spam filters in your email client. Gmail and Outlook have built-in protection — make sure it's activated.
